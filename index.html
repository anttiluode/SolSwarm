Your webcam is the world. Scouts decide where to look. An online Oja learner builds the frame's colour mean and main colour axis from what the scouts see.

Gate B: the swarm's view of the world vs the world's own

The scouts are drawn to a lure (click the live view to move it). Both learners get the same looks. Naive Oja learns the colours of wherever the scouts gathered. Importance-weighted Oja (weight = p/q) learns the colours of the whole frame. Each panel after the first redraws the frame using only that learner's mean + main axis (a rank-1 colour image).

Gate C: three samplers, same number of looks

All three are importance-weighted, so all learn the world's axis. The difference is how fast they track it. Uniform looks everywhere equally. Repo trace is SolSwarm v0's rule: trace += update size on every visit, so visited places attract more visits. Estimate trace keeps a per-site running estimate of update size, so it looks where a look is expected to teach something. Colour shows each sampler's current proposal q. Gate A: where a write would change the future most

A population lives on a coarse grid: each generation every cell multiplies it by exp(α·s), then it diffuses. Raise α and the population concentrates. Here s is the estimate-trace sampler's usefulness map plus your own writes. Right eigenvector v = where the population settles (the sender). Left eigenvector u = how much each cell's contents matter for future growth (the listener). Their product u·v is the elasticity: exactly d log λ / d si ÷ α. Click to write trace into a cell. The page checks the prediction α·ei·write against a full recomputation.

For small writes the prediction and the recomputation agree (first-order eigenvalue perturbation, the same formula as matrix-population elasticity analysis). For large writes they separate, because the response is nonlinear. Equal writes to different addresses have different effects. That is SolSwarm's hub-vs-leaf result, here on a live map.
Claim boundary: nothing here is a new algorithm. Oja's rule, importance sampling, variance-aware proposals and Perron eigenvalue sensitivity are all established. The page makes the three SolSwarm gates visible on a real, changing world. Camera frames never leave the browser.
