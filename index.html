Gate C / negative boundary

Bias correction is not a free optimizer

The closed-loop scouts leave trace where the raw Oja update is large, and that trace attracts later looks. Importance correction makes this adaptive stream much less biased, but at equal budget it still loses to uniform sampling.

uniform3.25°
adaptive + corrected7.49°
adaptive naive17.44°

What survives: nonuniform looking can be statistically corrected. What does not survive: “follow useful-looking updates” automatically beats randomness.
